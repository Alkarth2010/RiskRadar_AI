RISKRADAR AI - FRAUD INVESTIGATION POLICY
Policy ID: RR-DEV-004
Version: 1.9
Effective: 2025-02-28
Category: Device Fingerprinting & New Payment Instrument Risk

1. PURPOSE
Control risk from new or unrecognized devices and newly added payment methods.

2. DEVICE TRUST LEVELS
- Trusted: Seen in last 30 days + successful 2FA
- Known: Seen in last 90 days
- New: First appearance for customer
- High-Risk: Previously linked to fraud on any account (device graph)
- Suspicious: Signals consistent with emulation or automation

3. NEW PAYMENT INSTRUMENT RULES
Any transaction using a card/UPI/bank account added < 72 hours ago:
- +25 minimum risk points
- Mandatory step-up authentication (OTP + push) unless pre-whitelisted
- 48-hour cooling period for high-value or international use

4. COMBINED TRIGGERS (AUTO-HOLD)
- New device + new payment method + amount > ₹7,500
- New device + Tier-2/3 country transaction
- Device change + velocity cluster in same hour

5. AI AGENT INSTRUCTIONS
- Compare new device fingerprint similarity to all known customer devices
- Check device graph for use on other accounts
- Surface exact device registration time vs transaction time
- Recommend: mark trusted, require step-up, block + force password reset, or escalate
