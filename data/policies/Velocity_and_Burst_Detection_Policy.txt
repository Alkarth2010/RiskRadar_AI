RISKRADAR AI - FRAUD INVESTIGATION POLICY
Policy ID: RR-VEL-002
Version: 3.0
Effective: 2024-12-15
Category: Velocity & Burst Pattern Detection

1. PURPOSE
Detect rapid transaction sequences indicative of account takeover, card testing, or automated attacks.

2. VELOCITY WINDOWS & LIMITS
- 5 minutes: >3 transactions OR cumulative value > ₹12,000
- 30 minutes: >5 transactions OR cumulative value > ₹30,000
- 2 hours: >10 transactions OR cumulative value > ₹60,000
- 24 hours: >20 transactions OR value > 5x customer's 30-day daily average

3. HIGH-RISK PATTERNS
- Card testing: 4+ small (<₹300) attempts in <15 minutes, especially after declines
- ATO burst: Sudden activity after 7+ days of dormancy with >600% velocity increase
- Money mule layering: Rapid movement across multiple merchant categories in <90 minutes

4. EXEMPTIONS
Recurring mandates, salary credits, verified bill payments, and intra-account transfers do not trigger velocity alerts alone.

5. AI AGENT GUIDANCE
When velocity is detected, the agent must:
- Rebuild exact chronological timeline
- Distinguish card-testing (small incremental) vs ATO (large erratic) patterns
- Retrieve similar historical velocity cases via RAG
- Recommend hold + step-up auth or block with clear justification
