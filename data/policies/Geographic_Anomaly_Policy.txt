RISKRADAR AI - FRAUD INVESTIGATION POLICY
Policy ID: RR-GEO-003
Version: 2.1
Effective: 2025-03-05
Category: Geographic & Travel Anomaly Detection

1. PURPOSE
Identify transactions that violate reasonable travel or location expectations based on customer history.

2. RISK TIERS
- Low: Within 150km of last known location or declared home cities
- Medium: Different Indian city >400km away with <5hr travel window
- High: First-time international transaction
- Critical: Impossible travel (e.g., Mumbai to New York in <7 hours)

3. IMPOSSIBLE TRAVEL CALCULATION
Minimum flight time + 4 hours buffer (check-in, immigration, ground transport). Any pair violating this rule receives +40 risk points and hold recommendation.

4. COUNTRY TIERS
- Tier 1 (Low): India, Singapore, UAE, USA, UK, Germany, Japan, Australia, Canada
- Tier 2 (Elevated): Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, South Africa, Brazil
- Tier 3 (Critical): Nigeria, Pakistan, Russia, sanctioned jurisdictions

Tier 3 international transactions always require Level-2 review.

5. AI REQUIREMENTS
- Retrieve customer's full 90-day location history
- Calculate exact impossible-travel pairs
- Check for VPN/proxy indicators
- Surface prior travel declarations from CRM
- Recommend "Legitimate travel", "Hold for callback", or "Block" with supporting evidence
