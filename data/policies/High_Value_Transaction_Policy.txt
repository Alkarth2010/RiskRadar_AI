RISKRADAR AI - FRAUD INVESTIGATION POLICY
Policy ID: RR-HV-001
Version: 2.4
Effective: 2025-01-10
Category: High-Value Transaction Monitoring

1. PURPOSE
Define risk thresholds and investigation triggers for high-value transactions that exceed normal customer behavior.

2. THRESHOLDS
- Alert Level 1: Single transaction > ₹25,000 or equivalent foreign currency
- Alert Level 2: Single transaction > ₹50,000
- Alert Level 3: Single transaction > ₹1,00,000 (mandatory manual + AI review)

3. AUTOMATIC RISK BOOST SIGNALS
- Amount > 3x customer's 90-day average transaction value
- New payment instrument added in last 72 hours
- International merchant or location with no prior history
- Transaction between 00:00-05:30 IST
- Customer account age < 60 days

4. REQUIRED INVESTIGATION STEPS
- Pull customer 90-day transaction velocity and location history
- Verify device fingerprint against known devices
- Cross-check merchant risk score
- Review recent KYC / address change activity

5. ESCALATION
- Risk Score 50-70: AI recommendation, auto-approve if clean
- Risk Score 71-85: Level-1 analyst review within 20 minutes
- Risk Score >85: Immediate hold + Level-2 review + customer outreach

6. AI AGENT RULES
The AI must always cite specific policy clauses and surface similar past resolved cases. Never recommend block without at least two independent risk signals.
